## Farming Meets Technology Section

**Headline:**  
Farming meets technology.

**Lead Copy:**  
Good farming today uses information as well as hard work. Learners find out what each tool does, why it helps, and how to use it safely.

*OPTIONAL: List the tools actually in use or planned first.*

### Technology Topics

**1. Fertilizer and Soil**  
Soil tests and nutrient apps show what the soil really needs, so only the right amount is used. Learn how precision tools cut waste and protect water.

**2. Pests and Weeds**  
Scouting apps and photo identification spot problems early. Learn integrated pest management, and how targeted spraying, including GPS and drone tools, reduces chemical use.

**3. Crops and Water**  
Weather data, soil moisture sensors and timed drip irrigation help decide when to plant and water.

**4. Livestock Care**  
Digital records for animal health, feed, eggs and milk. Electronic tags, automatic waterers and feeders, and sensors or cameras that watch housing conditions.

**5. Food Preservation**  
Temperature monitoring and record keeping to store eggs and goat milk safely.

**6. Farm Business**  
Record apps, budgeting spreadsheets and online selling show how technology turns work into income.

### Safety Note

**Safety first.** Young children learn the ideas and watch demonstrations. Adults handle all fertilizers, pesticides, herbicides and machinery. Older youth practice only under trained supervision with protective gear. We are adding tools step by step as the farm grows.

### Visitor Safety Rules

These rules keep children, adults and animals safe. We will confirm the details with each group when a visit is booked.

#### Before the Visit

- A teacher or group leader books the visit and brings enough adult chaperones. We will confirm how many when you book.
- Please tell us in advance about allergies, medical needs, mobility needs and anything else that affects safety.
- Children should wear closed-toe shoes and clothes suited to the weather. Anyone who is ill should stay home.
- Photos of children are taken only with written consent from a parent or guardian.

#### During the Visit

- Everyone signs in, stays with their group and follows the farm guide.
- Children never go near animals, pens or equipment alone. Animals are touched only when a guide says it is safe.
- No running, climbing on fences or entering pens, and no feeding animals unless a guide gives you the food.
- No food or drinks near the animals. Everyone washes their hands after touching animals, soil or gates, and before eating.
- Fertilizers, pesticides, herbicides, tools and machinery are handled by trained adults only. Children watch demonstrations from a safe distance.
- Personal pets are not allowed.

#### In an Emergency

Our guides know where the first aid kit, exits and emergency numbers are. Follow the guide's directions, and keep your group together. Adults should tell a guide right away about any injury, illness or safety concern.
