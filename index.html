Growing roots, growing futures.

Aganze Growing Roots Farm is building a small family farm in Normal, Illinois where children and young people can learn how food is grown, how technology supports healthy farming, and how entrepreneurship creates opportunity.

We combine practical farm skills with money literacy, leadership, and community action so youth can grow confidence, resilience, and opportunity where they live.

A family farm in Normal, Illinois.

Aganze Growing Roots Farm is a small, family-run farm designed to teach youth entrepreneurship, sustainable agriculture, and self-sufficiency through hands-on learning.

Many young people grow up without direct contact with food production, animal care, or the practical decisions behind running a small farm business. Our farm gives them a place to understand where food comes from and how local systems can support opportunity.

We believe local youth can build meaningful livelihoods in their own communities when education is rooted in land stewardship, real work, and local opportunity.

Farming meets technology.

Good farming today uses information as well as practical skill. Learners discover what each tool does, why it matters, and how it is used safely.

Fertilizer and soil

Soil tests and nutrient apps show what the land needs so products are used carefully and responsibly.

Pests and weeds

Scouting apps and photo identification help spot problems early and support integrated management.

Crops and water

Weather data, soil moisture sensors, and timed irrigation help decide when and how to plant and water.

Livestock care

Digital records for feed, health, eggs, and milk support better animal welfare and farm decisions.

Food preservation

Temperature monitoring and record keeping help store eggs and goat milk safely and efficiently.

Farm business

Record apps, budgeting spreadsheets, and online sales help transform farm work into income.

Safety first: young children learn the ideas and watch demonstrations. Adults handle all fertilizers, pesticides, equipment, and farm machinery. We teach responsible, age-appropriate practices at every step.

Youth program: one year, three steps.

For young people aged 16 to 30. Applications open when the farm is ready to begin. Applicants are welcome regardless of prior experience.

Months 1 to 4

Learn the basics

Soil, crops, water, and harvesting on a small personal crop plot.

Months 5 to 8

Start a business

Plan an agri-business using digital tools and climate-smart methods.

Months 9 to 12

Lead your community

Run a community project and graduate with a certificate.

What you will learn.

Farming

Healthy soil, crop production, water management, and safe storage of food after harvest.

Technology

Farm apps, weather tools, mobile money, and online selling.

Money

Pricing, record keeping, saving, and writing a simple business plan.

Leadership

Speaking up, solving problems together, and leading community projects.

Who can join.

  • Aged 16 to 30 (under 18: parent or guardian consent)
  • Living in a rural or peri-urban community
  • Able to read and write at a basic level
  • Ready to learn, participate, and help others

No formal exams or qualifications are required. Girls and young women, youth with disabilities, and vulnerable youth will receive priority consideration.

What you get.

  • Hands-on learning on a working farm
  • Practical training in agriculture and technology
  • Skills in business, money, and leadership
  • Certificate recognition at the end of the program

The program fee is based on a sliding scale according to family income, with support options available where needed.

Visitor safety rules

These rules keep children, adults and animals safe. Details will be confirmed with each group when a visit is booked.

  • Teachers or group leaders should book visits and bring enough adult chaperones.
  • Please tell us in advance about allergies, access needs, or medical concerns.
  • Everyone signs in and stays with their group while visiting the farm.
  • Children never go near animals, pens, or equipment alone. Animals are touched only with supervision.
